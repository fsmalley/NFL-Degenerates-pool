How to Submit Your PickPlayer instructions
  1. Select the NFL week.
  2. Enter your player name.
  3. Enter your Survivor PIN. On your first submission, use the PIN you want associated with your player name.
  4. Select one available NFL team for that week.
  5. Review your selection and click Submit Pick.
  6. Confirm the website reports that your pick was saved successfully.

To change a pick: return before the applicable lock, enter your player information, select the replacement team, and submit again.
