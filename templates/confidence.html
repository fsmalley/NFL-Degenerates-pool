How to Submit Your Weekly PicksPlayer instructions
  1. Select the NFL week.
  2. Enter your player name and Confidence PIN. On your first submission, use the PIN you want associated with your player name.

  6. Review the entire entry and click Submit Week Entry.
  7. Confirm the website reports that your entry was saved successfully.
