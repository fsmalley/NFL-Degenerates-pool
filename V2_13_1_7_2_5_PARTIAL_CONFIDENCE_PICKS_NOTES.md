# V2.13.1.7.2.5 — Partial Confidence Picks + Individual Game Lock/Reveal

- Players may save one or more Confidence picks without completing the full week.
- Each saved pick must include a team and a unique confidence value.
- Saved confidence values remain reserved across the week, including locked games.
- Each game locks individually at its scheduled kickoff; missed games cannot be backfilled.
- Picks for a game become visible to all players at that game's scheduled kickoff while future-game picks stay private.
- Future games remain editable until their own kickoff.
- Tiebreaker is optional during partial saves and locks at the final scheduled game's kickoff.
- No database migration or environment variable change.
